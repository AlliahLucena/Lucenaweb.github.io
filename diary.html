First Year Moments

My first year at CSTA was both happy and nerve because I did not have any close friends yet. I also did not know anyone on campus, so I was still adjusting. It felt like a culture shock since college life was very different. A few weeks later, I was able to make some close friends, and until now, we are still together. My college life became lighter and more enjoyable because they are there for me. We support each other in everything.

Surviving Exam Week

Every exam week, there are many tasks to complete. Everything feels mixed in my mind because there are so many requirements to submit, and sometimes they even have the same deadlines. There are also many quizzes and laboratory activities, which makes the week very stressful for me. However, despite all the pressure, I remind myself that I need to stay strong and do my best. I am motivated to keep going because someone is supporting my education. In the end, I see these challenges as part of my growth, and I know that I will be able to overcome them.

First Year Cheerdance

During my first year, I joined cheerdance for the first time, and it was a very enjoyable experience. I met many friends, especially fellow first-year students, although most of my teammates were from higher levels. It made me happy because even though we had daily practices from morning until evening, there was always someone to talk to. Even though we did not expect to become champions, we knew in ourselves that we gave our best. During our actual performance, we experienced a technical problem when our music suddenly stopped. Despite this challenge, we remained focused and continued our routine. In the end, we were still able to achieve first place, which made the experience even more memorable and rewarding.

Second Year Cheerdance

During our cheerdance last March 2, I felt very happy because I was able to meet many new people. Some of my classmates, whom I was not very close with before, also became my friends because of this experience. It was also during this cheerdance that I met my kind and caring girlfriend, Shaira. This year’s cheerdance felt light and enjoyable under the guidance of Coach King. Even though we only had three weeks to prepare, everything felt manageable. Despite feeling tired after every practice, we still found time to laugh together. Although we did not achieve the championship as a team, we consider ourselves champions in terms of the memories we created, the laughter we shared, and the bond we built with one another..

Reflection for first year

Looking back at my first semester, I feel proud of how far I have come. At first, college felt overwhelming because everything was new, and I did not know anyone on campus. However, as time passed, I was able to find people who became my friends and support system. Because of them, my college life became lighter and more meaningful. There were also stressful moments, especially during exam weeks, when many tasks, quizzes, and laboratory activities had to be completed at the same time. Despite the pressure, I continued to stay strong and reminded myself of my purpose. I also experienced memorable moments, such as joining cheerdance, where I met many new people, strengthened my friendships, and created meaningful connections. These experiences helped me grow not only as a student but also as a person. I have realized that while my journey in college is my own, having friends who support and walk with me makes everything more enjoyable.

Second Year Moments

Now, I am already in my second year at CSTA, and I will be in my third year this coming June. Time has passed so quickly. It feels like it was only yesterday when I was a first-year student, feeling very anxious because everything was new to me. Even now, I still feel anxious at times, but it is no longer as overwhelming as before. This is because I already have friends and people I can be with in everything. Having them with me makes my college journey more comfortable and less difficult.
